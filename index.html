Our Story

A table rooted in Marathi soil, set with the world's flavours.

Basalt began with a simple belief — that vegetarian food, when treated with reverence, becomes art. Born in the lanes of Maharashtra and trained in the kitchens of the world, our chefs weave Kolhapuri fire, Konkan coast freshness and global technique into one unhurried dining journey.

Every dish is cooked from scratch, every spice is hand-picked from small farms, and every plate is served with the warmth of a Marathi home — only finer.

— The Basalt Family
